Halifax Planet

A virtual city connected to the real one. This is a concept, with no playable release yet.

The concept

  • Your place in the city — an avatar, a home, quests, levels and achievements.
  • Real weather — Halifax weather would shape the virtual world.
  • Real prices — fuel and energy data would influence city life in the game.
  • Real local stories — news and events from AIMeta Press would inspire activities.
Concept stage

Reality inspires play.

The idea is to turn local information into a shared experience: a city you can explore, understand and feel part of.
